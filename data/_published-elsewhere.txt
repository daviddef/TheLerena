# *** a warning that fires on every build is a warning nobody reads. ***

# *** AN ENTRY HERE IS A CLAIM THAT SOMEBODY LOOKED. *** It carries where the finding
# was published, so the claim can be checked rather than taken on trust. Adding a line
# to silence a report, without looking, is the one way to make this file a liability.
#

#
# *** THE QUOTED PHRASE IS WHAT MAKES THE CLAIM CHECKABLE. *** The tool reads the named

# a line here named a page that did not carry the finding at all; under this rule both

# Request — Western Cape Archives and Records Service (KAB)

**Target:** the **Death Notice** in the deceased estate file of **Robert Paul Lerena**, died 19 March 1950.

**Reference:** `MOOC 6/9/17017`, system 01, reference **1847/50**, part 1
(Master of the Supreme Court / Master of the Orphan Chamber series, Cape Town Archives Repository).
NAAIRS description: *"LERENA, ROBERT PAUL. ESTATE PAPERS."*, 1950.

**Repository:** Western Cape Archives and Records Service, 72 Roeland Street, Cape Town 8001.

## Why this document

The First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account from this same file is already held, and it shows
under fees paid to the attorneys **Dichmont & Dichmont**:

> "fee preparing **Death Notice**, Inventory and obtaining Letters of Administration — £2 2s"

So the Death Notice exists in the file. It is not among the pages held. A South African death notice
(Form A / Schedule 1) records, at question 19, **the names of the deceased's parents** — which no document
yet found does for this man.

## What to ask for

Copies of **the complete file**, but specifically:
1. **The Death Notice** — for question 19, names of parents, and for the stated date and place of birth.
2. The **Inventory** — which would list the horses, the stables and any land.
3. The **Will**, dated **6 February 1942**.
4. Any **correspondence** about the deceased's nationality or Argentine origin. He was an Argentine
   national (see below), so the estate may have involved the Argentine consulate.

## First, a correction to the brief
These are **not army records.** The letter already sent to the **Archivo General del Ejército**
(8 September 2026, msg `1a0811e9ee49a43e`) asks for his **libreta de enrolamiento** — his military
*enrolment*, which is a different thing entirely.

**Expulsions under Ley 4144, the *Ley de Residencia*, were administered by the Ministerio del Interior and
the police**, and the surviving files sit with the **Archivo General de la Nación**, not the army. The two
requests are complementary, not duplicates.

## Why ask at all
David's mother repeated to him, in September 2026, the family story that Pablo Armando
**"ran away from Argentina as he was a communist on the run."** It is the only story the family carries
that explains a *departure*, and a reason to leave is exactly what the record has never supplied.

The label needs correcting — the Argentine Communist Party dates from **1918**, and he left around
**1903–05**, when the movement was **anarchist and anarcho-syndicalist**. The testable mechanism is
**Ley 4144 (November 1902)**, which allowed deportation of **foreign-born** agitators without trial, and
which was used hard in exactly those years. **Rosario** — great river port, rail head, large dock and
railway workforce — was among the most militant anarchist cities in the country.

**And the sting is in the word *foreign-born*.** An Argentine national could be prosecuted but not deported
under Ley 4144. So if he was genuinely *expelled*, **he was not Argentine-born** — which would explain, at
a stroke, why three years of the Rosario baptism index do not contain him.

## Two alternatives if the AGN portal leads nowhere
- **Archivo Histórico de la Provincia de Santa Fe** and the **provincial police holdings** — a Rosario
  arrest would be recorded provincially before it ever reached Buenos Aires.
- **International Institute of Social History, Amsterdam**, which holds the major Argentine anarchist
  collections including runs of ***La Protesta***. The anarchist press **named** militants who were
  arrested and deported. The IISH does answer email, and this may in practice be the easier door.

# Request — South African racing records

**Target:** the registration of **Pablo Armando Lerena / Robert Paul Lerena** as a **trainer** and
**racehorse owner** at the Cape, c. 1911–1950.

## Why

Two independent documents call him a horseman, and a third places his stables:

- **Argentine passport, 24 Dec 1940** — *Profesión: **Trainer***; and military service in the Argentine
  **Guardia Nacional, 6º de Caballería**, 1915.
- **Death certificate, 19 Mar 1950** — occupation ***Race Horse Owner***.
- **Cape Town municipal building file, 1931** — *"plans of proposed **STABLES**, Koeberg Road, **Rugby**.
  RP Lerena"* (KAB 3/CT 4/2/1/3/474, ref B796).

South African racing press independently states that **Gavin Lerena's great-grandfather and grandfather
were trainers** — on the family's chart, those are this man and his son Ricardo.

## What to ask for, and where

1. **National Horseracing Authority of Southern Africa** (formerly the **Jockey Club of South Africa**),
   Turffontein, Johannesburg — the licensing and registration body.
   - **Trainers' licences** and **owners' registrations**, c. 1911–1950, for LERENA (also try **LERINA**).
   - **Registered racing colours** for the owner.
   - Any record of **horses imported from Argentina**, with country of origin.
2. **South African Stud Book** (Bloemfontein) — breeder/owner entries and import registrations.
3. **Western Province / Cape Turf Club and Milnerton Turf Club** records — race cards and results, Cape
   meetings. Rugby, where his stables were, adjoins **Milnerton**.
4. **Gold Circle** (Durban) for KwaZulu-Natal meetings, and the **Durban July** archive — a Chappell
   connection is documented there (see below).

## The Chappell thread

The racing press names **Frederick Chapell**, a jockey who trained **Migraine**, winner of the
**1957 Durban July**, as a "great uncle" of the present Lerena racing family. The Corpus Christi, Wynberg
baptism register records **Roque Luis Armando Lerena** marrying **Rosalina Wilhelmina CHAPPELL** on
**21 September 1936** at St Joseph's, Johannesburg. The two are almost certainly the same kindred, and
Chapell/Chappell records may carry Lerena detail with them.

## Newspapers

Owners' names, colours and race results were printed weekly. Worth searching:
- **Cape Argus** and **Cape Times**, 1910s–1950s. (The Cape Argus already appears in this family's
  records — the estate advertised for creditors in it.)
- **The Star** and **Rand Daily Mail** for the Transvaal generation.
- The **Sporting Post** archive — note its website blocks automated access and needs reading by hand.

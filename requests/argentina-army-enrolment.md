# Request — Archivo General del Ejército, Buenos Aires

**Target:** the **libreta de enrolamiento / legajo de servicio militar** of **PABLO ARMANDO LERENA**,
born Rosario de Santa Fe, 22 February 1882 — **clase 1882**.

**Why it is the best lead we have:** his own Argentine passport of 1940 records that he held a
*libreta de enrolamiento*, and gives his service as **Guardia Nacional, 6º de Caballería, año 1915**.
Enrolment was compulsory under **Ley 4031 (1901)**; the libreta itself under **Ley 8129 (4 July 1911)**.
**An Argentine enrolment record names the man's parents and his birthplace** — which is exactly what this
archive lacks.

**Contact:** Archivo General del Ejército, Buenos Aires. Accepts reference enquiries by email,
Mon–Fri 08:00–13:00.

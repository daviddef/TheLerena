## Why this is now the strongest lead in the project

Four candidate birthplaces have been tested and all four have failed:

- **Rosario cathedral**, read letter by letter under *any* surname for **1881, 1882 and 1883** — the
  cathedral being the only parish in Rosario until 1888;
- **Argentina generally** — two Lerena baptisms in the whole indexed country;
- **Uruguayan civil registration**, which began in 1879, with 1882 Lerena births present as a control;
- **Spain**, at index level.

There was **no provincial civil registry in Santa Fe in 1882**, and he is absent from the **1895 national
census**. So the documents he himself produced in 1940 to prove his nationality may be **the only
surviving statement of his parentage**.

## The four things asked
1. Does the Archive hold the **matrícula consular / registro de matrícula de argentinos** of the Cape Town
   consulate for 1930–1950, and does Pablo Armando Lerena appear in it?
2. Does the **1940 passport file** survive — and specifically, **what did he present to prove Argentine
   nationality**, does it name **his parents**, and does it give his exact birthplace?
3. Is the **consular correspondence** of Cape Town for that period held, and what is the procedure and
   cost for copies?
4. *(minor, and flagged as such)* Any easy way to check whether he ever held a **consular appointment** —
   the family tradition of a "Consul General" — in the *Guías Diplomáticas y Consulares* or personnel
   registers. The letter explicitly says not to spend staff time on this if it needs a real search.

The letter states plainly that this is a genealogical enquiry rather than academic research, offers to
write in English, and offers to pay any fees.

## Note on the consulship question
Asking it here is cheap and it tests a claim this archive has never been able to settle. The hypotheses
page records the suspicion that the tradition **grew out of the passport itself** — a consular document
that could easily be remembered a generation later as evidence of a consular *post*. The Cancillería is
the one body that could confirm or kill it outright.

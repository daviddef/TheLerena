## Why Amsterdam, for an Argentine question
Because the **Archivo General de la Nación has no email address** and its only remote channel is an
account-gated portal (see `agn-ley-4144-expulsiones.md`), while **the IISH answers email** — and it holds
one of the world's major collections of Latin American anarchist material. This is the same question by an
easier door.

**The anarchist press named names.** Papers like *La Protesta* reported arrests, imprisonments and
deportations of individual militants, often in lists. If Pablo Armando was caught up in the Ley 4144
expulsions of 1902–06, the press is at least as likely to have recorded it as the surviving state files —
and considerably more likely to be reachable from Australia.

## The four things asked
1. Does the Institute hold **Argentine anarchist and syndicalist periodicals for 1902–06** — *La Protesta*,
   and *La Protesta Humana* before it — in what form, and is any of it **text-searchable**?
2. Do they hold **labour or anarchist papers specific to Rosario or Santa Fe province** for that period?
3. Do the Argentine holdings include **FORA records, prisoners'/solidarity committee papers, or lists,
   reports or correspondence naming militants arrested, imprisoned or deported under Ley 4144**?
4. Is there a **name index**, and can a surname search be done remotely — by staff on a fee basis, or by a

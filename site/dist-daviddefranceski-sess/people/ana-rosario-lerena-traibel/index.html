Ana Rosario LERENA Traibel

Daughter of Avelino Lerena and Ambrosia Traibel, baptised at San Francisco de Asis on 12 March 1858 with her brother Alfredo Justiniano. THEY WERE NOT TWINS, AND THIS ROW SAID THEY PROBABLY WERE UNTIL 20 SEPTEMBER 2026. Both acts were re-read off the image that day - film 007713686 image 2847 - and each states its own birth. Hers: "que nacio el dia CATORCE de ENERO ... cincuenta y cinco" - 14 JANUARY 1855. Her brother's: 2 October 1856. They are twenty-one months apart and were carried to the font together, she at three years old and he at seventeen months. "Probably twins" was an inference from a shared baptism date, and the acts refute it. THIS FAMILY BAPTISED IN BATCHES, YEARS LATE, MORE THAN ONCE: Avelino's son Alejandro was christened at La Mauricia in January 1846 among a group of children of various ages, and here are two more, three years and seventeen months old. A granddaughter of Sancho. Her godparents were CONSTANTINO and ANA LAVALLEJA. READ OFF THE PLATE 21 September 2026, where this row had rested on a MACHINE TRANSCRIPT. Film 007713686 image 02847, folio 176, San Francisco de Asis - the two acts of 12 March 1858 that attach the Lerena Traibel branch to Sancho. EVERY NAME ON THEM SURVIVES THE CHECK, word for word. AND HER BIRTH DATE IS NOW READ, NOT TRANSCRIBED: 'que nacio el dia CATORCE DE ENERO de mil ocho cientos cincuenta y cinco, hija legitima de Don Avelino Lerena y de Dona Ambrosia Traibel, NATURALES DE ESTA REPUBLICA.'

The other archives

The Lerena Archive is one of seven, each following a different line of the same family. They use the same method and the same five words for how sure a thing is.

  • The Defranceschi Archive Istria · Kvarner · Carnia A family written down under eight sovereigns, in four languages, and spelled at least nine different ways. Defranceschi · De Franceschi · Defranceski · Defrančeski
  • The Falco Archive Arienzo, near Naples Nine documented generations born, married and buried in one valley, and then a migrant ship to Brisbane. Falco · Zampiello · Morgillo · Crisci · Liguori · Maione · Rivetti · Annecchino
  • The Blažević Archive Senj and the Krmpotska mountains An evidence-first archive for the family of Hedviga Blažević of Senj — carriers, a tobacco factory, and a wall grave. Blažević · Papić · Vukelić · Žubrinić · Kalanj
  • The Booyzen Archive Graaff-Reinet · Cradock · Johannesburg A Cape Dutch family that kept marrying British women, changed one letter of its name in a war, and came off the train at Johannesburg with nothing. Booyzen · Booysen · Kolbe · Barry · Downing · van Jaarsveld · Schoeman
  • The D'Arcy Archive England and Ireland · Parramatta · Brisbane Out with the 39th Regiment in 1825, and four generations afterwards in two Brisbane cemeteries. D'Arcy · Saniger · Sneyd · Hornby · Wakefield · Atkinson · Murdoch
  • The Mazza Archive Sicily and Calabria · Brisbane Four Italian quarters — Mazza of Piedimonte Etneo, and Prostamo, Arena and Polistena of Calabria — ending in four graves in a row at Nudgee. Mazza · Prostamo · Arena · Polistena

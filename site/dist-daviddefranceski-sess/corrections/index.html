Corrections

Every claim this archive has published, or was about to publish, and then had to take back. It is the most useful page here, because an archive that never corrects itself is only telling you what it wanted to be true.

Genealogy fails in one direction. A wrong name, once written down, gets copied; a right name only gets found. So this page exists to make the wrongness expensive — every retraction is dated, graded, and says how it was caught, because the catching is the transferable part.

Nothing here has been quietly edited away. Where a page elsewhere on this site once said something different, it now says the corrected thing and says that it used to say something else.

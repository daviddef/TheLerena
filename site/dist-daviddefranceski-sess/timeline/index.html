The Timeline

Every dated thing this archive can stand behind, in order, with the record it rests on — and the handful of world events that explain why a Rosario horseman was at the Cape at all.

From Rosario to Newlands

18 fixed points · 1882–1950

Show
1882 The world

The Argentine Stud Book is created by the Jockey Club on 30 June - the year he was born.

on Turf histories. A coincidence, recorded as one.

22 February Life

Pablo Armando Lerena born at Rosario de Santa Fe.

on His own Argentine passport, 1940; corroborated by "Clase de 1882" on the army enrolment card.

October The world

The South African War begins. Roughly 360,000 horses are shipped into the country; Argentina is one of the main sources.

on Remount-trade histories. He was 17.

31 May The world

The war ends. About 600 Afrikaners emigrate to Argentina - the traffic ran both ways.

on Argentina-South Africa relations.

25 November Life

His eldest son Roque Luis Armando is born at Cape Town.

on Corpus Christi Wynberg baptism register, 1911.

1908 Document

He enrols for Argentine military service at the CAPE TOWN consulate. The card names his father and mother.

on Ficha de enrolamiento, Cajon 985 (Lera), image ark 3:1:3QHK-MQL4-NQWN.

12 June Document

Roque, born 1905, is baptised at Corpus Christi, Wynberg - three months before his parents' wedding.

on The baptism register, naming both parents.

3 September Document

He marries Mary Septima Taylor at St Dominic's, Wynberg. He is a HORSE-DEALER of Diep River; both are single; both sign their own names.

on Cape Province marriage register no. 160, ark 1:1:88RY-GF3Z.

1915 Document

Guardia Nacional, 6th Cavalry - the service the 1908 card enrolled him for.

on Annotation on the 1940 passport.

6 January Life

Ricardo JUAN CARLOS Lerena born at Claremont, Cape - carrying his Argentine grandfather's name.

on Named with this exact date in the 1950 estate.

6 October Life

Nuno Fernando Lerena born at Wynberg, Cape. David's grandfather.

on Named with this exact date in the 1950 estate.

1931 Document

Plans lodged for STABLES at Koeberg Road, Rugby.

on Cape Town municipal file, KAB 3/CT 4/2/1/3/474 B796.

15 November Life

Mary Septima dies at 34 Florence Road, Observatory, aged 51. Buried Maitland, grave 7719A.

on Cape Province civil records; Maitland interment register p.3215 entry 163.

November Document

Two of his sons appear in the LDS South African Mission church census - Ricardo at Claremont, Nuno at Wynberg.

on LDS Church Census Records (Worldwide), 1914-1960.

24 December Document

Argentine passport Serie A No. 07962, issued at Cape Town on Christmas Eve. Profession: Trainer. Widower. A scar on the forehead.

on The passport itself, in the family's papers.

10 September The world

Argentina and South Africa establish diplomatic relations - thirty-nine years AFTER the Cape Town consular office enrolled him.

on Argentina-South Africa relations.

19 March Life

He dies at 7 Forth Road, Newlands, of coronary thrombosis. Registered as "Robert Paul LERINA", Race Horse Owner, widower.

on Union of South Africa death certificate A 214036, entry W612/50.

1950 Document

Estate: gross 15,527 pounds 15s - about half a million today - including a claim for 26 pounds of CARROTS.

on First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account, MOOC 6/9/17017.

The other archives

The Lerena Archive is one of seven, each following a different line of the same family. They use the same method and the same five words for how sure a thing is.

  • The Defranceschi Archive Istria · Kvarner · Carnia A family written down under eight sovereigns, in four languages, and spelled at least nine different ways. Defranceschi · De Franceschi · Defranceski · Defrančeski
  • The Falco Archive Arienzo, near Naples Nine documented generations born, married and buried in one valley, and then a migrant ship to Brisbane. Falco · Zampiello · Morgillo · Crisci · Liguori · Maione · Rivetti · Annecchino
  • The Blažević Archive Senj and the Krmpotska mountains An evidence-first archive for the family of Hedviga Blažević of Senj — carriers, a tobacco factory, and a wall grave. Blažević · Papić · Vukelić · Žubrinić · Kalanj
  • The Booyzen Archive Graaff-Reinet · Cradock · Johannesburg A Cape Dutch family that kept marrying British women, changed one letter of its name in a war, and came off the train at Johannesburg with nothing. Booyzen · Booysen · Kolbe · Barry · Downing · van Jaarsveld · Schoeman
  • The D'Arcy Archive England and Ireland · Parramatta · Brisbane Out with the 39th Regiment in 1825, and four generations afterwards in two Brisbane cemeteries. D'Arcy · Saniger · Sneyd · Hornby · Wakefield · Atkinson · Murdoch
  • The Mazza Archive Sicily and Calabria · Brisbane Four Italian quarters — Mazza of Piedimonte Etneo, and Prostamo, Arena and Polistena of Calabria — ending in four graves in a row at Nudgee. Mazza · Prostamo · Arena · Polistena

And for children: Our Family — The same ten families, told for a six-year-old.

An evidence-first family archive for the Lerena family — of Rosario and the River Plate, of the Cape and the Rand, and of Brisbane. Every claim carries its confidence. Living people are named — name and relationship only, never a date, a place, a photograph or a record.

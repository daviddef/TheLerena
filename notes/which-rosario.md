# Which Rosario?

## A correction, made before it became an error
Last night this archive flagged ***Gabino L. Llerena*** — a groom in the Uruguayan parish registers
**"whose birthplace is given as ROSARIO"** — as a Rosario-born Llerena tying Argentina and Uruguay
together. **The record has now been opened, and it does not say that.**

Every other person named on it is **Uruguayan**:

> Solís Grande · Maldonado · Punta de Carretas · Nuestra Señora del Carmen La Mayor, **Cordón, Montevideo**
> · Monte Video · and *Rosario*

***So his "Rosario" is ROSARIO, COLONIA, URUGUAY*** — a town on the Río de la Plata about 120 km west of
Montevideo — **not Rosario de Santa Fe.** The entry is corrected on the register.

## And this is a caution the whole project needs
**Uruguay has its own Rosario.** Any Uruguayan record naming *Rosario* means the Colonia town unless it
says otherwise. Given how much of this family's evidence runs through Uruguay — the highest density of the
surname in the world, the Trinidad kindred in Flores, the Montevideo households, a family note that the
consulship may have been **Uruguayan** — that ambiguity has been sitting in the middle of the problem
unremarked.

### The hypothesis it raises, and the reason it probably fails
If any part of the family's memory of *"Rosario"* came down through a Uruguayan branch, **the town could be
the wrong one entirely**, and every hour spent in the Santa Fe cathedral registers would have been spent in
the wrong country.

**But the passport is against it.** Pablo Armando's own Argentine passport of 1940 records his birth at
***"Rosario de Santa Fe"*** — naming the province, not just the town. That is his own document, and it is
explicit. **The hypothesis is recorded, and it is weak.** It would take something much stronger than an
ambiguity to overturn a passport.

## What the search did find: a Llerena family in Colonia
A third Uruguayan cluster, after Trinidad in Flores and Montevideo:

- **Tomás LLERENA** m. **María Morales** → *Vicenta Llerena Morales*, b. 1861, d. Carmelo, Colonia, 1927
- **Pedro LLERENA** m. **Sofía Clavijo** → *Susana Llerena*, b. **1884**, d. 1908
- **María LLERENA** m. **Antonio Medero** → *Petrona Medero*; also *Leoncia Mederos*
- **Ramón LLERENA**, named on Gabino's marriage record — most likely his father
- **Gabino L. LLERENA**, of Rosario, Colonia, *soltero* at his marriage
- **Manuel Medero Llerena**, m. Colonia 1926 · **Isabel Llarena** · **Manuel Rodríguez Llarena**

**Pedro Llerena**, fathering a daughter in **1884**, is precisely the generation that could have fathered a
son in 1882.

***But there is still no Pablo Armando.*** Uruguay has now been searched for him across civil registration,
church records and baptisms, in Flores, Montevideo, San José and now Colonia, and he is in none of it.

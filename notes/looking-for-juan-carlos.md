# Looking for Juan Carlos Lerena

*10 September 2026, the same day the name was found. Four searches, four negatives, and one of them
closes an instrument the archive had been holding in reserve.*

## 1. No marriage for him in Argentina

FamilySearch, surname **Lerena** (fuzzy), marriage in **Argentina**, **1865–1885** — the window in which
a man whose son was born in February 1882 would have married. **2,531 results, and not one Juan Carlos
Lerena.** The Argentine Lerena marriages of that window belong to families already met and already
placed elsewhere: Baldomero Llerena at Córdoba Cathedral (1876), the Llorens of Nogoyá and Bolívar, the
Larrena of Chascomús. Nothing at Rosario. Nothing in Santa Fe.

One new person: **Carolina Lerena**, mother of a groom named Julian Martinez, Buenos Aires civil
registration — recorded, unplaced.

## 2. No Juan Carlos Lerena of the right generation, anywhere in the Río de la Plata

Searched for any **Juan** Lerena born **1840–1865** — the plausible span for a man fathering a child in
1882. Ten thousand results across the world, and the Lerena Juans divide cleanly:

- **Mexico** — Jalisco above all, a large and entirely separate Lerena population (Juan Lerena m. Petra
  Arreola, m. Desideria Ramos; Norberto, Jacinto, Dionicio). Noted here for the first time.
- **Uruguay** — the Trinidad, Flores kindred: Alejandro Florencio Lerena m. Severa Fernández, their sons
  Juan Gualberto and Juan Alberto; Avelino Lerena m. Ambrosia Traibel; Juan Bernardo, son of Bernabé.
- **Spain** — one, in the Écija municipal census of 1903.

**Not one Juan Carlos.** He is not in any indexed record in Argentina or Uruguay.

## 3. Full-text search — run at last, and it cannot reach him

This was item five on two successive lists and had never actually been swept. It has now been swept, and
the answer is structural rather than negative.

FamilySearch's full-text search — the one instrument that reads **unindexed handwriting** — returns
Lerena in quantity, but the collections it returns are:

> **Arequipa, Peru** (an entire Lerena population, previously unknown to this archive) ·
> **Valladolid and Madrid, Spain** (nobility genealogies and notarial records) ·
> **Trinidad, Flores and Montevideo, Uruguay** · Jalisco, Mexico · a scatter of United States newspapers.

**Santa Fe is not in the corpus. Neither is Rosario.** Not thinly represented — absent. The instrument
held in reserve for the hardest question turns out not to cover the ground the question is on.

That is worth knowing precisely, and it retires the item rather than leaving it open as a hope.

A phrase search for **"Juan Carlos Lerena"** returns exactly two collections, both Montevideo, and both
are the same man: **Juan Carlos González Lerena**, dispensed from the three canonical banns on 8 November
1922 to marry Concepción Petrona Urbano. Born 1899. Not ours. The dispensation register was read to be
sure — it is a fine instrument and the wrong man.

## 4. The Cape estate images are still restricted

`3:1:3Q9M-C913-K9H3-D`, in *South Africa, Cape, Probate Records of the Master of the High Court,
1822–1990*, still returns **Image Restricted** while signed in. Re-tested, not assumed. The estate file
**MOOC 6/9/17017** cannot be read from here.

## What this leaves

**The Death Notice is now the most valuable unread document in the project — and it has a prediction
attached to it.**

A South African death notice records, at question 19, **the names of the deceased's parents**. We already
know the form works: Roque's 1973 notice gave *Roberto Lerena* and *Mary Septima Lerena*.

So the 1950 death notice of **Robert Paul Lerena** should say **Juan Carlos Lerena** and **María**.

That is a real test, not a fishing trip. If it agrees, the card is confirmed by a second document
written on the other side of the world by a different family in a different language. If María's entry
carries a **maiden name**, the question the card could not settle is settled in one line. And if it
disagrees, this archive needs to know that too.

The letter to the Western Cape Archives has been written and updated with this prediction. It has not
been sent.

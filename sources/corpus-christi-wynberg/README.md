# Corpus Christi Catholic Church, Wynberg — baptism register

**2 Clare Road, Wynberg 7800 · tel 021 761 3388**
Sourced by **Cheryl Defranceski**, directly from the parish archives.

Supplied to this archive as photographs (four crops of one entry) on 8 September 2026.
**The image file still needs to be added to this directory.**

## The entry

> Anno **1905** die **25** mensis **Nov.** natus est, et anno **1911** die **12** mensis **Junii**
> baptizatus est **Roque Lois Armando**, filius **Pablo Armando Lerena** et **Mary Septima Lerena
> (olim Taylor)** conjugum: a me **Bertram W. Glynn**.
> Patrinus fuit **José Breton** (proxy), Matrina fuit **Maria Louisa Breton** (proxy).

Later annotations on the same entry:

> Ipse anno **1936** die **21** mensis **Septembris** matrimonium contraxit cum **Rosalina Wilhelmina
> Chappell** in ecclesia **Sancti Joseph** apud **Johannesburg** — Rev. P. **McCarthy**.

> *(blue ink)* Roque L. Armando Lerena, **as widower**, was married a[t] **St Charles Chapel, Victory
> Park, Johannesburg** on **12-3-1966**.

## Why this entry is decisive

It names **Pablo Armando Lerena** as the father of Roque Luis Armando Lerena, and **Mary Septima
(olim Taylor)** as the mother. Roque's 1973 death notice names the same mother but gives the father as
"Roberto Lerena". **The two men are therefore one man.** See `notes/findings.md`.

`olim Taylor` also documents **Taylor** as Mary Septima's maiden name, which until now rested only on the
family's descendant chart.

## Note on the circulated translation

The English translation printed beneath the circulated image is a machine rendering and garbles the Latin —
it makes Pablo Armando the *son* of Roque and turns *Septima* into "seventh". The correct sense is
**"...was baptised Roque Lois Armando, son of Pablo Armando Lerena and Mary Septima Lerena (formerly
Taylor), spouses."** The transcription above is this archive's own reading.

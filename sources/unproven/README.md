# Unproven — not part of the documented line

## The San Miguel baptisms, 1872
`record-image_939D-R89W-WB.jpg` · `record-image_939D-R8P1-H.jpg`

*Libro de Bautismos de la Parroquia de San Miguel, Año de 1872* — two exposures of one opening.

Left entry: **Roberto Juan Roque LLORENS**, baptised 4 September 1872, born 16 August 1872, *hijo legítimo*
of **D. Roberto Llorens** (natural de España, 25) and **Da. Francisca Fernández** (natural de España, 23),
of Calle Chacabuco 156. Godparents Juan Berenguer and Rita Carbajo. Cura Manuel Velarde.
Right entry: Carolina Marciala Padín — an unrelated family.

**Status: UNPROVEN. David Defranceski states plainly that these records are not established as this
family's (8 September 2026), and this archive treats them accordingly.**

Why they were ever a candidate: the given names **Roberto** and **Roque** both recur in the documented
Lerena family, and the birth year is in roughly the right range for Roberto Paul Lerena (c.1874).

Why they are doubtful:
- The surname is **LLORENS**, not Lerena. Llorens is Catalan for Lawrence and is not a variant of Lerena.
- The street names (Chacabuco, Victoria, Piedras) and the San Miguel Arcángel parish point to
  **Buenos Aires**, not Rosario.
- Both parents are Spanish-born, which fits nothing yet established here.

**Not cited on the site. Kept only so the same record is not chased twice.**
